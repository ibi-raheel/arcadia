illumination

~ a gilded letter, for a beginning ~
drop cap · blue ground
W

elcome to the scriptorium. Here the first letter is gilded, pressed into lapis, bordered with crimson. It takes up three lines of text, and the rest of the paragraph flows around it like a river finding its channel.

drop cap · wax ground
O

n a wax-red ground, the gilt letter glows deeper. This variant suits chapter openings or heavier headings — where the ink wants to shout a little, but still with restraint.

drop cap · verdigris
T

hose aged by years of salt air sit on verdigris — the patina of bronze gone greenish-dark. Quieter than the lapis variant, it suits reflective or late-night text.
